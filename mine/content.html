Content Format Guide

Quick reference for shooting and editing each format. This is fixed guidance, not AI-generated — no key needed.

Reels / Short Video (TikTok, IG Reels, FB Reels)

  • Vertical 9:16, first 1-3 seconds must show the payoff or a bold hook — don't build up slowly
  • Ideal length: 7-15s for pure highlight clips, up to 30-45s if there's a story/tutorial angle
  • Cut on action — trim dead air aggressively, keep pacing fast throughout
  • Burn in captions/text even for gameplay-only clips — many watch muted
  • Use a trending audio at low volume under game audio when it fits, not forced
  • Cover frame: pick the single sharpest, most exciting moment — this is what stops the scroll before they even press play

Photo Posts / Carousels

  • Use 1:1 or 4:5 crop, not the game's raw widescreen — it gets cropped awkwardly otherwise
  • Slide 1 (cover) needs a bold text overlay or the most striking image — it decides if they tap through
  • 3-7 slides is the sweet spot; order best-to-worst won't work — put your second-best moment last to reward full swipes
  • End on a soft call-to-action slide (follow for more clips, drop your best play, etc.)

Stories / Status

  • 9:16, meant to feel raw and immediate — behind-the-scenes, polls, "which clip should I post today"
  • Use interactive stickers (poll, question, slider) — they boost engagement signal more than a static image
  • Post 2-4 per day max on active days; save your best moments for Highlights so new visitors see them first

Platform Fit

  • Facebook: native Reels + longer native video tend to outperform reposted/watermarked clips — this is likely part of your view swings
  • TikTok: pure short-form, highest posting frequency tolerance
  • Instagram: Reels for reach, Carousels for saves/shares, Stories for daily connection

Monetize

How gaming creators typically get paid on these platforms, and where to focus first.

Facebook

In-Stream Ads (ad revenue share inside your videos), Stars (viewer tipping, especially on Live), and Fan Subscriptions (recurring monthly perks). Requires a Professional/Creator account meeting Meta's current follower and watch-time thresholds.

TikTok

Creator Rewards Program (pays based on watch time and originality of longer videos), LIVE Gifts (viewer tipping during livestreams), and TikTok Series (paid exclusive content).

Instagram

Reels/content bonuses (where available), Subscriptions (paid exclusive content for your most loyal followers), and Badges during Live.

Brand Partnerships

Gaming brands, peripherals, and mobile game studios pay creators to feature their product — often the most accessible path early on since it depends on engagement and niche fit, not platform payout thresholds.
Eligibility numbers (followers, watch-minutes, region availability) change often and vary by platform and country. Always check your own Creator/Professional dashboard on each platform for current requirements.

Go Live

Plan a proper livestream: what to set up beforehand, and a ready script to guide you while you're on camera.

Before You Go Live

  • Test your stream setup (game audio + mic balance) at least 10 minutes before going live
  • Prep a short list of 3-4 talking points so there's never dead air
  • Post a heads-up on your other platforms 30-60 minutes before you go live
  • Pin a comment/caption telling viewers what you're playing and what to expect
  • Have a clear goal for the stream (ranked push, viewer Q&A, casual grind)
  • Keep the first 2 minutes welcoming — greet people by name as they join
  • Plan 2-3 moments to directly ask viewers a question to spark chat
  • Decide your closing line and call to action in advance (follow, next stream time, etc.)
  • Save the VOD and clip the best 3 moments for Reels/TikTok afterward
