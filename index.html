Step 06

Supply Lines — Monetization

How Facebook creators are typically paid, and where you should focus first.

In-Stream Ads

Short ads play inside your videos and you earn a share of that ad revenue. Requires a Professional/Creator account in good standing and meeting minimum followers and watch-time shown in your Meta Professional Dashboard.

Facebook Stars

Viewers send paid Stars during live videos or on posts as a form of tipping, and you get a share of their value. Eligibility is shown in your dashboard once you are set up as a creator.

Fan Subscriptions

Your most loyal followers pay a small recurring monthly fee for exclusive perks, extra content, or a badge that shows on their comments.

Brand Partnerships

Companies pay you to feature their product, disclosed using Meta's branded content tools. Often the most accessible path early on, since it depends on trust and engagement rather than Meta's payout thresholds.

Eligibility numbers (followers, watch-minutes, region availability) change and vary by country. Always check your own Meta Professional Dashboard for the current requirements.
